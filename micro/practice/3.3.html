Profit Maximization

Read the theory → Drill 3.3 →

A. Accounting vs. Economic Profit

Problem 1. A firm earns $200,000 in revenue. It pays $120,000 in explicit costs. The owner gave up a $70,000 salary and $10,000 in interest to run it. Find accounting and economic profit, and state whether the firm earns a normal profit.

Show solution

Accounting profit = 200,000 − 120,000 = $80,000.
Implicit costs = 70,000 + 10,000 = 80,000.
Economic profit = 80,000 − 80,000 = $0
→ the firm earns exactly a normal profit.

Problem 2. Why is economic profit never larger than accounting profit?

Show solution

Economic profit subtracts implicit (opportunity) costs in addition to explicit costs, and implicit costs are never negative, so economic profit ≤ accounting profit.

B. Measuring Profit from P and ATC

Problem 3. At its profit-maximizing output a firm sells Q = 100 at P = $20, with ATC = $14. Find total profit.

Show solution

π = (P − ATC) × Q = (20 − 14) × 100 = $600 profit.

Problem 4. A firm produces Q = 50 at P = $10 with ATC = $13. Find its profit or loss.

Show solution

π = (10 − 13) × 50 = −$150 (a loss), since P <
ATC.

Problem 5. A firm sells Q = 40 at P = $15, with total cost of $500. Find profit using TR − TC.

Show solution

TR = 15 × 40 = 600. π = 600 − 500 = $100 profit.

Problem 6. What is the profit-maximizing quantity?

Show solution

Produce while MR ≥ MC.
Units 1–3 qualify (18≥8, 16≥10, 14≥12); the 4th has MR 12 <
MC 14.
Profit-maximizing quantity = 3.

Problem 7. Why shouldn't the firm produce the 4th unit?

Show solution

Its marginal revenue (12) is less than its marginal cost (14), so the 4th unit reduces profit by 2.
The firm stops where MR = MC.
