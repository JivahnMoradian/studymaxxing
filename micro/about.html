The Four Sections, and Who They're for

Students learning or revising

Theory

A condensed summary of the key terms and ideas for each chapter. Most pages add a college-level section that goes deeper.

Students who need reps

Practice

Dozens of practice questions per chapter, with full solutions you reveal only when you're ready. Repetition is how econ clicks.

Exam prep

Drills

Classic quantitative problem types, 50 fresh questions each, that check your answer instantly. You won't run out of practice.

Professionals & the curious

In Real Life

Plain-English explanations of what each topic means in everyday life, with no graphs or equations.

How to Use the Site

  • Pick and choose. This platform is deliberately broad, covering more than the AP syllabus or any single course. Study the topics relevant to you and skip the rest.
  • Different system? If you're sitting the IB, A-Levels or another exam, the material won't map perfectly to your format, but most of the concepts, problems and drills still apply. Again, pick and choose.
  • Jump between chapters with the dropdown at the top of Practice and In Real Life (and the chapter picker in Drills); the ‹ › arrows step through them in order.
  • In Theory, expand the "Go deeper: college-level" blocks for more depth, and open the blue boxes to check your understanding.
  • In Drills, hit Shuffle for a fresh set of 10 or Show all for every question; your answers are checked instantly.
  • In Real Life pages carry a relevance tag (high / medium / low) so you can see at a glance how much a topic matters outside the exam.
  • Cramming? If your exam is in 24 hours, stick to Theory, Practice and Drills, and save In Real Life for later.
