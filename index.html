About

The Mission

"This class is confusing" is something I've heard from more students than I can count. Between confusing curricula, uneven teaching and a shortage of practice problems, it's no wonder so many people decide a subject just isn't for them.

This site is my attempt to fix that: to make each subject clearer, easier and far better supplied with practice, for students anywhere, for free. The courses cover the US AP syllabus and most US college-level intro courses, with extra depth for those who want it.

About Me

My name is Jivahn Moradian. I am a teacher, tutor and coach. I grew up in France to an American mother and an Indian father, and survived the French Baccalauréat. I studied Economics at Princeton University (summa cum laude), then earned a Master's in Innovation, Entrepreneurship & Management from Imperial College London, graduating top of my class.

Today I run my own tutoring and coaching business, and have taught hundreds of students economics, accounting, finance, math and more. I also work part-time as a project manager at a French AI startup. In my spare time I enjoy hiking in the mountains and traveling to adventurous locations, such as Uzbekistan and Bolivia.

A New Approach to Learning

Creating a comprehensive curriculum with thousands of practice problems and targeted exercises has traditionally required a large team and years of development. New AI capabilities are opening up a different possibility: combining deep subject knowledge and teaching experience with the power to develop, test, and refine educational material at an unprecedented scale.

This project brings decades of teaching experience together with cutting-edge AI to create an extensive, carefully structured collection of learning resources. The technology makes it possible to generate and explore far more variations, examples, and practice opportunities than traditional development methods would allow. Each page, however, is guided, reviewed, and verified through human expertise. AI expands what's possible; sound educational judgment determines what belongs.

This is an evolving approach to educational design, and part of its promise lies in continually improving what it can offer. If you encounter an error, an unclear explanation, or an exercise that doesn't work as intended, please let me know. Your feedback will help make this resource more accurate, effective, and useful for every student.
